Honour "don't show again today" on every popup, and match the picker mock

Two things from the client's deck: the footer checkbox did nothing for most
popups, and the store-picker card look was off.

- `isPopupDue` only read the suppression timestamp for "once a day" popups,
  so the store prompt (frequency "every") came straight back after a reload
  or in a new session even after the visitor ticked "Don't show again
  today". The visitor's explicit choice now wins for 24 h on every
  frequency (covered by a new test).
- The picker cards follow the mock: an optional flag/logo beside the store
  name (uploaded per store in /admin/site), the visitor's own store tinted
  with its accent and marked Current / "You are here", and the other
  store's button quiet gray (#E2E2E2) instead of a second purple CTA.
- The popup footer is the mock's checkbox + Close pair instead of two text
  buttons.
